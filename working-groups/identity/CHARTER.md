## What We're Building

AI agents are rapidly becoming essential parts of software systems, but today there's no standard way to identify them, verify their credentials, or manage their permissions. The Identity Working Group is creating the foundational standards that will enable secure, trustworthy identity for AI agents operating across different platforms and ecosystems.

**Why this matters:** As AI agents take on more responsibilities—from booking travel to managing infrastructure—we need robust identity standards to ensure they can be trusted, audited, and held accountable. Our work enables the future of safe, interoperable multi-agent systems.

## Mission

Our mission is to define comprehensive standards for identity in multi-agentic systems—ensuring that agents can securely identify themselves, interact with trust, and operate seamlessly across organizational boundaries.

## Goals

The goals of this working group are:

- **Define identity mechanisms** — Enable the assignment and onboarding of identities for agents, resources, and tools in an open, interoperable way.
- **Ensure collision-free identity use** — Prevent conflicts in identity assignment and usage across multi-agentic systems.
- **Establish trust frameworks** — Define methods for trustworthy assignment, onboarding, presentation, verification, and utilization of identities, including mechanisms to authenticate identifiers and their provenance.
- **Address multi-modal interactions** — Cover identity requirements for human-to-agent, agent-to-tool, agent-to-agent, and agent-to-human interactions.
- **Promote interoperability** — Enable seamless integration with Identity Providers (IdPs), Authorization Servers, and standard authentication/authorization flows across ecosystems.

## Scope

This working group is responsible for all identity-related standards in multi-agentic systems. Our work is developed openly in the [Identity repository](https://github.com/agntcy/identity), where specifications, proposals, and reference implementations are maintained.

## Active Workstreams and Roadmap

The working group is currently advancing several key initiatives:

- **Dynamic client ID metadata integration** — Standards for client metadata in agentic systems
- **Context sharing mechanisms** — Protocols for sharing identity context across agents
- **Audit logs and authorization frameworks** — Authorization models and audit capabilities
- **Agentic identity interconnect protocols** — Cross-system identity interoperability
- **Brokered identity gateways** — Gateway patterns for identity brokering
- **Design principles for agentic identity** — Foundational principles and best practices

Detailed roadmap items, timelines, and progress updates are discussed in our regular meetings and documented in our shared resources.

## How to Get Involved

We welcome contributions from everyone, regardless of experience level! Here are multiple ways you can participate:

### 1. 💬 Join the Slack Channel
Start by joining the AGNTCY Slack ([get your invite here](https://join.slack.com/t/agntcy/shared_invite/zt-34sxmw5e8-LqlUxxcxROq3HRb56QSkUg)) and then join [#identity-wg](https://agntcy.slack.com/archives/C08TZPX83KR). Introduce yourself, ask questions, and connect with the community. This is the fastest way to get oriented and start contributing.

### 2. 📅 Attend a Meeting
Join our regular working group meetings. Check the [calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=admin@ops.agntcy.org) for upcoming sessions. Meetings are open to all, and you can participate as much or as little as you're comfortable with.

### 3. 📂 Explore Resources
Browse our [shared Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1TuWnu991AhtpoqFdBjxQdEbb6VadVbBO) to review current specifications, meeting notes, and proposals. This will help you understand ongoing work and identify where you might contribute.

### 4. 🎯 Contribute to a Workstream
Interested in a specific area? Review the [workstream leads](#leadership-and-contributors) and reach out via Slack or during a meeting. They can help you find the right starting point based on your interests and expertise.

### 5. 🔧 Provide Feedback or Submit PRs
Review open issues and pull requests in the [Identity repository](https://github.com/agntcy/identity). You can provide feedback on proposals, submit improvements, or propose new ideas. All contributions are valued!
